Judgment Infrastructure for Human-Led AI

AI should help people think better, not quietly train them to outsource judgment.

The most useful agent workflows do not start with a perfect prompt. They start with context, uncertainty, disagreement, stakes, and a person trying to make sense of what matters.

That is the layer I am building for: portable skills that help people organize the mess, pressure-test the thinking, deliberate consequential decisions, and test ideas, claims, and decisions before trusting them without surrendering agency to the tool.

Judgment infrastructure means the AI is not the decision-maker. It is the scaffold around better human judgment: clearer inputs, sharper critique, more visible trade-offs, better questions, and smaller evidence-seeking actions.

The Briefing Room turns scattered context into a brief a human can think with. Ground Truth challenges the claim, plan, or draft instead of validating it too easily. The Quorum brings multiple expert lenses to decisions where smart people could reasonably disagree. Test Drive turns ideas, claims, and decisions into small trials that create evidence before commitment.

The goal is not faster content for its own sake. The goal is more capable people: clearer, more creative, more honest, and more agentic in their own thinking.

Have messy context? Start with The Briefing Room.
Have a claim, plan, or draft? Use Ground Truth.
Have a consequential decision? Convene The Quorum.
Have an idea, claim, or decision? Try it with Test Drive.
