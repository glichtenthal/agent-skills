Where it fits in an AI build harness

A human-led operating layer for AI-assisted builds.

AI Project Steward works alongside your model, coding and deployment tools, and project context. It keeps consequential decisions, approval boundaries, and verification visible from the initial request through a working result.

It does not replace your runtime, permissions, secrets, or observability systems; it helps the human and AI use those components coherently.

Five-step operating flow

A repeatable way to steward an AI build.

The skill introduces structure where it matters without turning every implementation detail into a meeting.

What changes

The difference is in the collaboration, not just the output.

Without a steward

AI can efficiently move from request to artifact, while the work around the artifact stays implicit.

  • Important trade-offs emerge late or not at all.
  • Approval boundaries can blur as the work accelerates.
  • Earlier choices may disappear between sessions.
  • “Done” can mean output exists, not that the real use case works.

With AI Project Steward

The human and AI collaborate on the build as a whole, with decisions and verification kept in view.

  • Material options and trade-offs are surfaced before they lock in.
  • Actions needing permission are held for the right approval.
  • Accepted decisions are recorded for the next session.
  • Completion is checked against the real scenario, not assumed.
