# Agent Skills Catalog Guidance

## Visual finish

- Treat a one- or two-word fragment at the end of a cohesive headline, promise, or label as a visual QA defect. Prefer a small width or type adjustment, or keep the final phrase together with a nonbreaking span; do not create horizontal overflow on narrow screens.
- Keep back-navigation labels calm and legible: use the supported Instrument Sans weight range, normal letter spacing, and avoid synthetic-heavy type.
- Treat an unusually large gap between a header or hero action area and its first content section as a visual QA defect. Keep hierarchy clear, but avoid stacking oversized bottom padding and section margins that make a page feel longer without adding meaning.
