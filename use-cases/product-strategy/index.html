Product Strategy

Use judgment infrastructure when product work is full of customer feedback, roadmap pressure, analytics ambiguity, and team disagreement.

Start with the workflowSee the full demo

Use This When

  • Customer feedback is noisy and teams disagree about what it means.
  • A roadmap bet sounds plausible but the evidence is mixed.
  • You need to decide whether to build, pause, simplify, reposition, or test.

Recommended Workflow

Start with The Briefing Room to structure the context. Use Ground Truth to challenge the diagnosis. Bring in The Quorum if the roadmap decision has real trade-offs. Use Test Drive to define the smallest credible evidence loop.

Use The Briefing Room on this product strategy context. Separate facts, claims, assumptions, tensions, open questions, and what this is ready for next.

Example Product Questions

  • Are we solving the right customer problem?
  • Is this feature request a signal or a loud anecdote?
  • Should we rebuild onboarding or run a narrower diagnostic first?
  • What evidence would make this roadmap bet more or less trustworthy?

Skills To Use

The Briefing Room for synthesis, Ground Truth for critique, The Quorum for trade-offs, and Test Drive for evidence design.
