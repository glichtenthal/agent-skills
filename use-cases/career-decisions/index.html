Career Decisions

Use judgment infrastructure when a career choice has context, emotion, trade-offs, and uncertainty that a normal pros-and-cons list cannot hold.

Open The QuorumOpen Test Drive

Use This When

  • You are weighing an offer, role change, relocation, sabbatical, or new project.
  • You want clearer thinking without outsourcing the decision.
  • You need to test assumptions about fit, growth, money, risk, energy, or identity.

Recommended Workflow

Use The Briefing Room if your context is scattered. Use Ground Truth to challenge the story. Use The Quorum for the decision. Use Test Drive to design a small experiment before committing.

Use The Quorum on this career decision. Preserve disagreement, name the real trade-offs, and give me a calibrated recommendation with a pre-mortem.

Example Career Questions

  • Should I take this offer or keep looking?
  • Am I reacting to short-term frustration or a real pattern?
  • What would I need to learn before making a bigger move?
  • What small experiment could test whether this path fits?

Skills To Use

The Briefing Room for context, Ground Truth for honest critique, The Quorum for trade-offs, and Test Drive for small experiments.
