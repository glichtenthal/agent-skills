Founder Decisions

Use judgment infrastructure when the decision is consequential, emotionally loaded, and easy to rationalize after the fact.

Start with the workflowOpen The Quorum

Use This When

  • You are considering a pivot, strategic bet, senior hire, fundraise, or product direction.
  • You need a decision process that preserves dissent instead of flattening it.
  • You want AI to sharpen your judgment, not give you permission.

Recommended Workflow

Use The Briefing Room if the context is messy. Use Ground Truth to challenge the story you are telling yourself. Use The Quorum for the decision. Use Test Drive if the call needs evidence before commitment.

Use The Quorum on this founder decision. Preserve disagreement, name the real trade-offs, give me a calibrated recommendation, and include a pre-mortem.

Example Founder Questions

  • Should we pivot or keep pushing the current strategy?
  • Should I hire this senior candidate now or wait?
  • Is this fundraising narrative actually strong or just polished?
  • What would change my mind before I commit the company?

Skills To Use

Ground Truth for anti-sycophancy, The Quorum for high-stakes trade-offs, and Test Drive for evidence before commitment.
