The Judgment Loop

1

Brief

The Briefing Room separates the known, claimed, assumed, tense, unresolved, and useful.

2

Critique

Ground Truth names the weakest assumption, strongest counterargument, and missing evidence.

3

Deliberate

The Quorum convenes multiple lenses when the decision has real trade-offs.

4

Test

Test Drive turns a plausible idea, claim, or decision into an evidence-seeking trial.

You can enter the loop anywhere. A clean brief may go straight to action. A high-stakes decision may need The Quorum. A claim that only needs evidence can go directly to Test Drive.
